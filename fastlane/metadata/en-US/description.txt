NanoKVM turns your iPhone, iPad or Mac into a remote screen, keyboard and mouse for a Sipeed NanoKVM.

• Low-latency H.264 / H.265 video of the target computer
• Keyboard and mouse, including a trackpad mode on iPhone and iPad
• Custom EDID matched to your display for a sharp, 1:1 picture
• Mount ISO and disk images, Wake-on-LAN, paste text
• Manage Wi-Fi, Tailscale, SSH, firmware updates and more on the KVM
• Devices are found automatically on your network; credentials stay in your iCloud Keychain

A NanoKVM device is required.
